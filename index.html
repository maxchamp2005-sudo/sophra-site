The idea behind Sophra

A budgeting app made for real people.

Sophra was created to make personal finance feel more approachable. Many budgeting tools overwhelm users with too much information or too many features at once. Sophra takes a different approach by making the experience cleaner, more visual, and easier to follow.

The goal is to help users build better habits over time, understand where their money goes, and feel more in control of everyday financial decisions.
